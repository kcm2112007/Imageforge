Background Remover

Remove backgrounds using an on-device AI model that runs locally in your browser.

Your images stay on your device. Processing happens locally in your browser.

How to use this tool

1

Upload

Drag one image in, or click “Choose Image.”

2

Remove background

Click the blue “Remove Background” button. The first time, it briefly downloads a small AI helper — this can take 10–30 seconds.

3

Pick a background

Choose transparent, white, black, or your own color.

4

Download

Click “Download PNG” to save your image with the background removed.

How this works: the first time you remove a background, your browser downloads a small on-device segmentation model (roughly 15–40 MB depending on the model tier) from a public CDN (jsdelivr.net / huggingface.co) so it can run entirely on your device afterward. Your photo itself is never uploaded anywhere — only the general-purpose model file is downloaded, once, and then cached by your browser for next time. This requires an internet connection the first time; processing after that works offline.

About ImageForge

ImageForge is a collection of simple browser-based image tools designed to make everyday image processing easier.

Our mission

"Make useful image tools fast, simple and privacy-friendly."

We built ImageForge because everyday tasks like shrinking a photo before emailing it, resizing a graphic for social media, or cropping a headshot shouldn't require installing software or handing your files over to a stranger's server. Every tool here runs using your browser's own image-processing capabilities.

Privacy first

Images are processed locally whenever technically possible. Nothing is uploaded by default.

No friction

No account, no install, no watermark. Open a tool and start working immediately.

Honest engineering

If a browser can't do something reliably, we say so instead of faking it.

Who it's for

Bloggers, students, small business owners, developers and anyone who needs quick, reliable image tools without a learning curve — compressing a photo for a website, resizing a thumbnail for YouTube, or cropping an image to a specific aspect ratio.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Local-first processing

ImageForge is built around one principle: your images are yours. Whenever your browser supports it, all image processing — compressing, resizing, converting, cropping and background removal — happens entirely on your own device using standard browser APIs (such as the Canvas API) or an on-device AI model. Your image files are not uploaded to our servers as part of normal tool operation.

No account required

You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use ImageForge's tools.

No intentional server-side image storage

ImageForge does not intentionally collect, store, or retain copies of the images you process. Because tools run client-side, we generally have no access to your image content at all.

Local storage usage

ImageForge uses your browser's localStorage to remember small preferences on your device, such as your selected theme (light, dark, or system). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

External resources

Some tools — specifically the Background Remover — may download an on-device AI model file from a third-party content delivery network (CDN) the first time you use that feature, so the removal can run locally in your browser. This is a one-time model download, not an image upload; your photo itself is never sent anywhere as part of this process. We clearly disclose this on the Background Remover page before any download begins.

The site's fonts, icons and styling are self-contained and do not rely on tracking scripts or third-party advertising networks.

No selling of personal information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or image data to third parties.

Analytics

If ImageForge ever adds analytics in the future, this policy will be updated to disclose exactly what is collected and why.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
